# BRIEF · Why this vault exists

Write this before anything else. If you cannot fill it in, you are not ready to build agents.

## The goal

*Example (fictional):* Build a small set of agents that run real GTM work for **Acme Analytics**: research accounts, draft outbound, qualify replies, prepare calls and keep the CRM honest, with a human deciding rather than executing.

## Why a vault comes first

A model already knows generic B2B GTM. Average advice executed at scale produces average results. The vault supplies what the model does not have:

| The gap | What the vault holds |
|---|---|
| Which practices survived contact with our market | L1 conclusions, each with an ID, a confidence level and a traceable source |
| Our own numbers | A P0 evidence layer that outranks every market conclusion |
| Provenance | Every P0 claim tagged `verified`, `reported` or `inferred` |
| What we say inconsistently | A register of facts we state differently depending on who is in the room |
| Permission to say "I don't know" | Explicit gap sections in every topic |

## What this vault is not

- Not a content library.
- Not a substitute for talking to customers.
- Not finished knowledge.
- Not a decision. It makes decisions cheaper to take.

## How we will know it worked

| Test | Status |

## What the evidence has already changed

*Fill this in after P0 is built. It is the section to read if you read nothing else.*

*Fictional example:* Acme's P0 showed that sending more email produced a lower reply rate, while referrals produced most closed-won revenue with no owner or tracking. `inferred` Acme does not have a throughput problem, it has a hit-rate problem. The agent build was redirected from "send more" to "find the message and the channel that work".
